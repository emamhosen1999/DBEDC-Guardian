Derived state — NOT recomputed by this command
  late / early / overtime minutes and day status: SAFE. AttendanceStatusService computes them at read
    time from punchin/punchout and stores nothing, so correcting the punches corrects the reports.

  attendances.symbol: NOT derived from punch times. It is a leave/manual-presence marker written by
    the admin screens and the leave flow; the biometric path never sets it. Unaffected by this shift.

  policy_status / needs_approval / policy_exception_reason: may be STALE after this correction.
    PunchPolicyGuard::assess() ran at ingest against the device's WRONG (too-late) time, and
    AttendancePunchService persisted the verdict on the row. Correcting the punch does not re-run it.

    The exposure is narrower than "everything is now wrong", and the conditions are exact:
      · assess() is called from ONE place, punchIn(), and reads the punch-in moment only. A row whose
        punch-OUT moved and whose punch-in did not cannot have a stale verdict.
      · punch_strictness = 'warn' (the default, and the value when no attendance_policies row matches)
        persists nothing at all — the out-of-window case returns a transient UI warning. NOT stale.
      · punch_strictness = 'flag' marks every punch provisional regardless of its time. Time-independent,
        so shifting the punch cannot change it. NOT stale. Reason string: "flagged by policy".
      · punch_strictness = 'restrict' is the only time-dependent verdict: provisional iff the punch-in
        falls outside [shift.start - outside_window_minutes, shift.end + outside_window_minutes].
        Reason string: "outside permitted window". THIS is what goes stale, in both directions —
        a punch flagged only because 09:00 was stored as 11:00, and a genuinely out-of-window punch
        that the +2 h skew happened to push INSIDE the window and so was never flagged.
